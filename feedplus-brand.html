05 — Philosophy

Design Principles

01 Tool, Not Decoration Every pixel, line, and color must earn its place through function. If it does not inform hierarchy or readability, it does not exist.
02 Precision Over Noise Data is the asset. Present it with surgical clarity. Eliminate visual interference between the viewer and the information.
03 Broadcast Engineering Designed for operators under pressure. High contrast, hard edges, consistent anchors, predictable layouts. No surprises on air.
04 Always Live Zero latency aesthetics. The interface must feel instantaneous — solid state, no transitions that delay. Speed is the feature.
